Privacy Policy

MKM Pocket · Effective date: 24 November 2026

App: MKM Pocket (Android package: com.mkmstudio.mkmpocket)
Developer: MKM Studio
Privacy contact: mkmgamingcompany@gmail.com

In brief: MKM Pocket is an offline-first personal finance app. The Android app stores the information you enter on your device and does not send it to MKM Studio. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. You control any export or sharing you start.

1. Information you enter or add

MKM Pocket lets you record financial and profile information, such as your name or business name, address, email address, phone number, accounts, balances, income, expenses, budgets, debts, transaction dates, categories, notes, reminders, and settings. You may also add profile pictures and receipt images. This information is stored locally in the app on your device.

2. Optional access to contacts and camera

  • Contacts: If you choose “Add from contacts” and grant permission, Android’s contact picker provides the contact you select. The selected contact details, such as name, phone number, or photo, may be copied into your local MKM Pocket records. The app does not upload your address book.
  • Camera: If you choose to take a receipt photo, camera access is used for that photo. The image is stored with the relevant local record. You can also choose an image or file using Android’s system picker.
  • Notifications: If enabled, notifications are used for reminders and budget alerts created in the app. They are scheduled on your device.

You can deny optional permissions and continue using the app; the related feature may be unavailable.

3. Shared messages and payment images

If you intentionally share a message or payment screenshot to MKM Pocket from another app, the app can process the selected text or image on your device to prepare a transaction for your review. Image text recognition runs on-device. An item is added to your records only through the app’s review flow. MKM Pocket does not read your entire messaging inbox.

4. Backups, exports, and sharing

When you create a backup or export, MKM Pocket creates a file on your device. Android backups are normally saved in a Downloads folder; exported files may contain your financial records in readable form. You choose whether to move or share a file, and which app or recipient receives it. If you send a file to email, cloud storage, messaging, or another service, that service’s own privacy terms apply. MKM Studio does not receive these files.

Some payment shortcuts can open a payment or banking app that you select. Information needed to prepare the payment, such as a recipient or amount, may be passed to that app. Review the selected provider’s terms before completing a payment.

5. Optional third-party backup or sync apps

MKM Pocket itself does not upload your records. If you separately install and choose to use a compatible Connector or backup app, that separate app may read a backup file you select and transfer it to a service or account you authorize, such as your private GitHub repository. The separate app and service then handle that data under their own privacy terms. Backup files are not encrypted by MKM Pocket; protect access to any location where you store them.

6. Data sent to MKM Studio

The Android release of MKM Pocket is designed to work offline and does not send the financial or profile records you enter to MKM Studio. The app does not include ads, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Opening an external website or payment app is an action you choose; the destination’s privacy policy applies to information you provide there.

7. Storage, security, and retention

App records are stored in the app’s private storage area protected by Android’s app sandbox. No storage method is completely risk-free, so use a device screen lock and keep backup files secure. Your records remain on the device until you delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Backup and export files saved outside the app may remain after uninstall and must be deleted separately. If Android device backup is enabled, Android or Google may handle eligible app data according to your device backup settings and their own policies.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete records in MKM Pocket. To remove the app’s local data, use Android Settings → Apps → MKM Pocket → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Delete any backup or export files separately from the folder or service where you saved them. MKM Pocket does not create an account with MKM Studio, so there is no MKM Studio account to close.

9. Children

MKM Pocket is a personal finance tool and is not designed for children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact MKM Studio at mkmgamingcompany@gmail.com or visit mkmstudio.org.
